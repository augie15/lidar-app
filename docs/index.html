Scan a room. Scan an object. Export in a few taps.

Tilda is a free, private iPhone app that turns your LiDAR scanner into a 3D scanner. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Get help

What it does

  • RoomScan one room or a whole floor. Get a clean 3D model, a floor plan, square footage and a photo tour.
  • ObjectPhotoreal textured models built on your iPhone, or a quick LiDAR shape for plain or shiny objects.
  • MeshRaw LiDAR as a mesh, a colored point cloud or color-coded surfaces.
  • ExportUSDZ, OBJ, STL and PLY, plus your photos with camera poses. Share through AirDrop, Files or Mail.
  • Apple IntelligenceOn supported iPhones, Tilda names and summarizes scans and answers questions about them. All on device.
  • Siri and ShortcutsStart a scan or open your latest one by voice, and find scans in Spotlight.

Private by design

Scans stay on your iPhone, in the Tilda folder you can see in the Files app. Tilda has no servers and makes no network calls. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to export or share a file. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

An iPhone with a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro models) running iOS 18 or later. Apple Intelligence features need iOS 26 or later on a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, and are optional.
