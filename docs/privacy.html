Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-23

Tilda is built so your scans stay yours. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store or receive any of your data. There is no Tilda server.
  • Camera, LiDAR, photos, scans and measurements are processed and stored only on your iPhone.
  • Data leaves your iPhone only when you export or share a file, or choose to send feedback (a public GitHub issue, or an email you send yourself).

Camera and LiDAR

Tilda asks for camera access so it can scan with the camera and LiDAR scanner. The camera image, depth data and photos are used on your iPhone to build 3D models, floor plans, measurements and photo tours. They are not sent to us or to anyone else by the app. You can turn camera access off in Settings, Tilda on your iPhone, but you will not be able to scan.

On-device processing

Everything Tilda does with your scans runs on your iPhone: building models, calculating room sizes and square footage, reading photos with Apple's Vision framework to suggest tags and detect text, and building the photo tour. Tilda uses only Apple's frameworks. It includes no third-party code, SDKs or analytics.

Tilda also adds your scans to Spotlight search on your iPhone, and offers shortcuts to Siri and the Shortcuts app through Apple's App Intents. This information is stored and handled by iOS on your device under Apple's own policies.

Apple Intelligence

On supported iPhones, Tilda can name and summarize scans and answer your questions about them using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. This runs entirely on your iPhone. Tilda does not use Apple's Private Cloud Compute or any other cloud model. You can turn off automatic naming and summaries in Settings.

Storage and deletion

Your scans, photos, measurements and summaries are stored in Tilda's Documents folder on your iPhone. You can see them in the Files app under On My iPhone, Tilda. Because Tilda has no servers, we have no copy of your data.

  • Deleting a scan in Tilda removes that scan's files from your iPhone and from Spotlight.
  • Deleting the Tilda app removes all of its scans and settings from your iPhone.
  • Files you already exported or shared are copies. Remove them where you saved or sent them.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or device backups, Apple may include app data in them according to your Apple settings.

Sharing and exports

When you export a scan (USDZ, OBJ, STL, PLY, photos with poses, photos for Mac, or a room summary PDF), Tilda opens the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app or service you choose, such as AirDrop, Files, Mail or Messages, and is governed by that service's terms and privacy policy. Tilda does not see where you send a file. Exported files can contain photos of your surroundings and the dimensions of your space, so check who you are sharing them with.

Feedback through GitHub or email

Settings has a Send feedback screen. Tilda itself sends nothing. When you tap to send, it opens a prefilled new-issue page on GitHub in an in-app browser (Safari View Controller), and you decide whether to submit. The page is prefilled with what you typed plus your Tilda version and build, iOS version and iPhone model. No scans, photos, files or personal data are included. You can edit or delete anything before submitting.

If you submit, the issue is public on GitHub, posted under your GitHub account (a free account is required), and handled under GitHub's Privacy Statement and terms. Do not include private information in a public issue.

You can instead choose to send feedback by email. That opens a draft in the iOS Mail app with the same details, and nothing is sent until you tap Send. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply and to fix the problem. We do not share them.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log visits to these pages under its own policies. These pages load no scripts, fonts, trackers or cookies of our own.

Children

Tilda does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by local law). It has no accounts, ads or purchases. The feedback page on GitHub is a public site that is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top. If the way Tilda handles data changes in a material way, we will say so in the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email brett@augiesrentals.ca, or open an issue at github.com/augie15/lidar-app/issues. Issues are public, so do not include private information.
